# Writing Ad Creative

Creative is where the angle meets the scroll. Start from a defined angle — if
there isn't one, use the `generating-sales-angles` skill first.

## Video scripts

Most paid social video is watched **on mute, on a phone, by someone who did not
ask to see it**. Write for that.

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | Earn the next 3 seconds. Motion, face, or the problem on screen. |
| Problem | 3–8s | Show the frustration, don't describe it. |
| Product | 8–15s | Enter as the resolution. |
| Demo | 15–25s | It working, in real conditions. The most persuasive footage you have. |
| Proof | 25–32s | Review on screen, or the result. |
| CTA | 32–40s | One instruction. Price or offer visible. |

Rules that decide whether it works:

- **The first frame is the ad.** A weak hook makes the rest irrelevant.
- **Burn in captions.** Assume no sound, always.
- **Vertical 9:16**, safe margins clear of platform UI.
- **Show, don't claim.** Footage of the product working outperforms any adjective.
- Write the script as a two-column shot list — visual on the left, spoken or
  on-screen text on the right — so it can actually be filmed.

## Ad copy

Produce **3–5 variants per angle**, differing in the *argument*, not the
adjectives. Rotating synonyms is not a test.

- Primary text: first line does the work — it's what shows before "See more"
- Headline: the offer or outcome, under 40 characters
- One clear CTA, matching the landing page's promise word for word

A mismatch between ad promise and landing headline is the most common and most
expensive leak in the funnel.

## WhatsApp sequences

WhatsApp converts well and bans fast. The rules are not optional — they are
enforced by Meta and by consumer law.

**Verified against Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform documentation (September 2026):**

- **Opt-in is mandatory.** You may only message someone who gave you their
  number *and* explicitly agreed to receive messages. A number from an order
  form is not consent to marketing.
- **Business-initiated conversations require a pre-approved message template.**
  Templates are reviewed by WhatsApp and can be paused or rejected at any time.
- **The 24-hour customer service window:** when a user messages you, a 24-hour
  timer starts and resets on each new user message. Inside it you may reply
  freely. **Outside it, only an approved template can reach them.**

Sequences that are legitimate and work:

| Message | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Order confirmation | Immediate | Expectation setting, reduces support load |
| Shipping update | On dispatch | The single biggest driver of "where is my order" tickets |
| Delivery check-in | 2–3 days after arrival | Catches problems before they become chargebacks |
| Review request | ~7 days after arrival | Proof for future creative |

Abandoned-cart and promotional messages need their own explicit opt-in and an
approved template. One easy unsubscribe, honoured immediately. No unsolicited
bulk messaging — that is what gets numbers banned permanently.

## Claims that get the account banned

Same list as angles, because creative is where they get smuggled in:

- Health, medical or weight-loss claims; before/after body imagery
- Income or earnings claims
- Invented statistics or fabricated studies
- Testimonials from people who aren't real customers
- Personal-attribute targeting — implying you know the viewer's health, body,
  finances or situation. Meta rejects this outright.
- Thumbnails or hooks that misrepresent what the product does

A banned ad account usually ends the business. Treat this list as a hard
constraint on creative, not as guidance to weigh against performance.

## Output

For each angle: one shot-listed video script, 3–5 ad copy variants, and — only
where opt-in exists — the WhatsApp sequence with each message marked as
template or in-window.
